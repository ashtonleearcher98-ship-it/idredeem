HELP & INFORMATION

Before you download

Where do I find my redemption code?

Your code is supplied by the Greenwick document issuer. If it is missing or does not work, contact the office that issued your documents.

How do I save my documents?

Open your collection, then select Download PNG under each document. On a phone, check your Downloads folder or save the opened image to your photos.

Can I open my collection again?

Yes. Your code allows repeat access to the same collection. The portal does not use one-time redemption.

What if my details are incorrect?

Contact your Greenwick document issuer for a corrected document. This portal provides the supplied documents without changing their details.

About these documents

This service supplies virtual documents issued for the Commonwealth of Greenwick micronation. Recognition by other authorities is not implied.

Privacy and access

There are no analytics or third-party scripts. Your code is processed in your browser and is not saved by this portal. Hosting providers may keep standard request logs. Anyone with your code can open the collection. Closing it removes the displayed documents from this page; downloaded files remain on your device.
